Reading **The Myth of Sisyphus by Albert Camus**.

This book might come as a surprise to anyone who believes that we are all here to serve some purpose in life. I held that belief for a very long time, only to encounter this book in 2026, and it has shifted something inside me. The fact that it was actually written in 1942, piques an indescribable curiosity in me about how, across generations, we seem to keep arriving at the same existential questions, rediscovering ideas that others have wrestled with long before us. 

The book talks a lot about absurdism. It emphasises the fact that the universe is indifferent to our endless quest for meaning. All this while, we thought we had figured out life, but it was all just a temporary diversion from something we should have been doing all along. 

And that is living. 

The whole point is to LIVE.
