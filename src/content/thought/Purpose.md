Reading **The Myth of Sisyphus by Albert Camus**.

This book might come as a surprise to existentialists who believe that we all are here to serve some purpose in life. I held that belief for a very long time, only to discover this idea, written in 1942, in 2026 and it has shifted something inside me. 

The book talks a lot about absurdism. It emphasises the fact that the universe is indifferent to our endless quest for meaning. All this while, we thought we had figured out life, but it was all just a temporary diversion from something we should have been doing all along. 

And that is living. 

The whole point is to LIVE.
